About Lakshmi Puja

Lakshmi Puja is dedicated to Goddess Lakshmi and is performed by devotees with prayers for abundance, well-being and gratitude. Customs may differ for Diwali, business-related worship and other occasions, so the ceremony format should be selected accordingly.

Whether you are observing a festival, marking a new business venture or celebrating a personal milestone, Upasana helps you coordinate the right arrangement with a suitable Panditji.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Puja Sthapana & Sankalp

Prepare the worship space and begin with customary prayers as per family tradition.

2
Ganesh Puja

Lord Ganesha may be worshipped at the beginning as per family custom.

3
Lakshmi Avahan

Prayers invite Goddess Lakshmi's blessings according to the selected tradition and vidhi.

4
Archana & Offerings

Flowers, lamps, naivedya, coins or other customary items may be offered as per tradition.

5
Account Books / Business Puja (If Requested)

For business worship, discuss appropriate customary additions in advance with the Panditji.

6
Aarti & Prarthana

Conclude with aarti and prayers for prosperity and well-being.
