About Shradh / Pitru Puja

Shradh and Pitru-related observances are performed in many Hindu traditions to remember and honor departed ancestors. Customs, dates, offerings and procedures vary substantially by region, family practice and the specific observance.

The appropriate ritual should be confirmed with a knowledgeable Panditji. Upasana handles your enquiry with sensitivity and discretion, helping you coordinate all aspects with care.

1
Sankalp

Begin with the customary resolve and details relevant to the observance and family tradition.

2
Invocation & Prayers

Prayers are offered according to the family's ritual tradition and the Panditji's guidance.

3
Tarpan (If Applicable)

Water offerings may be part of the selected rite, following proper guidance and tradition.

4
Pinda / Other Offerings (If Applicable)

Offerings may be included according to the observance and family tradition.

5
Brahmin Bhojan / Daan (If Customary)

Discuss whether these arrangements are part of your family's practice and who will coordinate them.

6
Closing Prayers

Conclude with customary prayers and respectful observance as per family tradition.
