About Shradh / Pitru Puja

Shradh is the sacred ritual of offering food, water and prayers to departed ancestors. Performed during Pitru Paksha (the 15-day lunar fortnight) or on death anniversaries, it is believed to bring peace to the souls of the departed and remove Pitru Dosha from the family.

The ceremony includes Tarpan (water offerings), Pinda Daan, and chanting of Pitru Sukta. Proper performance ensures the ancestors rest in peace and bless the living family members.
