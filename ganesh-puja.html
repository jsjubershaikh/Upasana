About Ganesh Puja

Ganesh Puja is performed before any new beginning — be it a business launch, house construction, wedding, or any important ceremony. Lord Ganesha, the first deity to be worshipped in Hinduism, bestows his devotees with wisdom, success and a path free of obstacles.

The puja includes Ganesh Shodashopachara, Ganesh Atharvashirsha chanting, and offerings of Modak, Durva grass, and red flowers.
