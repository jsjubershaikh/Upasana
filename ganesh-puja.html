About Ganesh Puja

Ganesh Puja is dedicated to Lord Ganesha, widely revered as the remover of obstacles and the deity invoked at the beginning of many auspicious activities. The ceremony may be performed independently or as part of a larger religious event.

Whether you are starting a new venture, celebrating a milestone or observing a festival, Upasana helps you coordinate the ceremony with the right Panditji and appropriate samagri for your occasion.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Sankalp

The devotee's intention is stated with customary prayers as guided by the Panditji.

2
Kalash / Puja Setup

The worship space and required items are prepared according to custom and family tradition.

3
Ganesh Avahan

Lord Ganesha is invoked through prayers and mantra as per the selected puja vidhi.

4
Offerings & Archana

Flowers, durva, modak or other offerings may be used according to tradition.

5
Mantra & Stuti

Ganesh mantras and devotional prayers are recited as selected and guided by the Panditji.

6
Aarti & Prasad

Conclude with aarti, prayer and prasad distribution as customary.
