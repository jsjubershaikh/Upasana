About Navgraha Puja

Navgraha Puja is a form of worship honoring the nine grahas recognized in Hindu tradition. Devotees may arrange it as a religious observance or following consultation with a knowledgeable priest or astrologer.

Specific procedures, sankalp and offerings vary by tradition and purpose. Upasana helps you coordinate all the requirements, confirm samagri and find a suitable Panditji for your ceremony.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Sankalp & Opening Prayers

Begin with sankalp and customary invocations as per the Panditji's guidance.

2
Kalash / Mandala Setup

The ritual arrangement is prepared according to the chosen vidhi and ceremony requirements.

3
Navgraha Avahan

Prayers are offered to the nine grahas as prescribed by the selected vidhi and tradition.

4
Mantra Jaap & Archana

Graha-specific mantras, names or offerings may be included as guided by the Panditji.

5
Havan (If Selected)

A homa may be included only when agreed upon and suitable for the venue and safety conditions.

6
Aarti & Shanti Prarthana

Conclude with customary prayers for peace, harmony and well-being.
