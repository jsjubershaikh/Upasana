About Navgraha Puja

Navgraha Puja is performed to appease the nine celestial bodies — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu — which govern different aspects of our lives according to Vedic astrology. Imbalances in these planets can cause health issues, financial problems, and relationship conflicts.

This puja includes individual homas for each graha, chanting of Navgraha Stotra and specific mantras with flower and grain offerings dedicated to each planet.
