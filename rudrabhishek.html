About Rudrabhishek Puja

Rudrabhishek is one of the most sacred and powerful Vedic rituals dedicated to Lord Shiva in his Rudra form. During this ceremony, the Shivalinga is ritually bathed (Abhishek) with sacred liquids like Panchamrut (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugar), Gangajal, sugarcane juice, and coconut water while chanting the potent Sri Rudram from the Yajurveda.

Performing Rudrabhishek washes away negative karma, brings health, prosperity, peace of mind, and protects the devotee and family from planetary afflictions.
