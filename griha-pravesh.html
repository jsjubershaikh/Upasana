About Griha Pravesh Puja

Griha Pravesh is a Hindu ceremony performed when entering a new home. Families offer prayers for auspicious beginnings, harmony and well-being in the household. Rituals can vary based on regional customs, the home's circumstances and the family's chosen tradition.

With Upasana, you can discuss the ceremony format, coordinate the required arrangements and confirm all details with a suitable Panditji before the ceremony day.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Sankalp & Initial Prayers

The ceremony may begin with sankalp and prayers to Lord Ganesha as per the family's custom.

2
Kalash Sthapana

A kalash may be prepared and placed according to the prescribed custom and Panditji's guidance.

3
Vastu Shanti / Vastu Puja

Where selected, prayers are offered in accordance with the agreed Vastu-related vidhi.

4
Ganesh & Graha Prayers

Additional prayers may be performed as recommended for the ceremony.

5
Havan / Homa

A sacred fire ritual may be included if requested and suitable for the venue — confirm in advance.

6
Aarti & Griha Prarthana

The ceremony concludes with customary prayers and blessings for the new home.
