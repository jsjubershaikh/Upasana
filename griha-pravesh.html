About Griha Pravesh Ceremony

Griha Pravesh is a sacred Hindu housewarming ceremony performed before moving into a new home. It purifies the living space, dispels negative forces, and pleases Vastu Purusha and the Kuldevta (family deity) to ensure happiness and prosperity for all family members.

The ritual includes Vastu Puja, Navgraha Shanti, Ganesh Puja, Boiling of Milk, and Havan ceremony.
