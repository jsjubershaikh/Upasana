About Havan / Homa

Havan or Homa is a fire ritual in which offerings are made into a consecrated fire while prayers and mantras are recited. The procedure and mantras depend on the purpose of the homa and the tradition followed.

The selected ritual must be suitable for the venue and comply with applicable safety rules. Upasana helps you discuss all requirements, confirm venue suitability and coordinate the Panditji and samagri in advance.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Sankalp

The purpose and customary details of the ceremony are stated with the Panditji's guidance.

2
Initial Prayers

Ganesh puja and other opening prayers may be performed before the havan begins.

3
Havan Kund Preparation

The ritual area and fire setup are prepared as prescribed and permitted for the venue.

4
Agni Pratishtha & Mantra

The sacred fire is invoked and mantras are recited as appropriate to the selected homa.

5
Ahuti

Offerings are made with prescribed mantras according to the selected homa and tradition.

6
Purnahuti & Closing Prayers

The ritual concludes with customary final offerings and prayers.
