About Namkaran Sanskar

Namkaran Sanskar is a traditional Hindu naming ceremony for a child. Families gather to offer prayers and formally celebrate the child's name. The timing, naming method and rituals vary across communities and family traditions.

Upasana helps you coordinate the ceremony with a suitable Panditji, discuss the ritual sequence and plan the event according to your family's customs and comfort.

Key Rituals / Service Steps
1
Sankalp & Blessings

The ceremony begins with customary prayers and blessings for the child and family.

2
Ganesh Puja

Opening worship may be performed as part of the family's custom and tradition.

3
Kalash / Devata Prayers

Additional ritual preparations may be included according to the chosen vidhi.

4
Naming Ritual

The child's name may be ceremonially announced or whispered as per family tradition.

5
Family Blessings

Parents and elders offer blessings and prayers for the child's wellbeing and future.

6
Aarti & Conclusion

Conclude with customary prayers, aarti and prasad if included in your arrangement.
