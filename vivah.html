About Vivah Sanskar

Vivah Sanskar is one of the sixteen sacred Sanskars in Hindu tradition. It marks the beginning of a new chapter in a couple's life, bringing together two families through sacred vows, Vedic mantras and traditional rituals.

The ceremony celebrates commitment, mutual respect, family values and a shared journey through life. With Upasana, you can arrange your wedding Panditji and discuss the rituals and samagri required according to your family tradition.

Key Rituals Included
1
Kanyadaan & Panigrahan

The sacred ritual of entrusting the bride and accepting one another's hands in marriage.

2
Agni Pradakshina (Pheras)

The couple takes sacred rounds around the holy fire as part of the wedding ceremony.

3
Saptapadi (Seven Sacred Steps)

Seven steps representing the couple's shared commitments and journey together.

4
Mangalsutra & Sindoor

Traditional wedding rites symbolizing the marital bond, according to the family's customs.

5
Laja Homa & Parikrama

Offerings are made into the sacred fire as part of the traditional wedding rites.

6
Blessings & Final Rituals

The ceremony concludes with customary prayers and blessings from family and elders.
